## BACK COVER COPY

Nobody tells you, when the first attempt fails, that failure was never the final answer — only the first draft of one. *Retries* is a collection of honest, unglamorous stories about people who tried, failed, and tried again — in love, in work, in the ordinary business of building a life — until the version that finally worked showed up.

This is not a book about celebrities or extraordinary luck. It is a book about ordinary persistence, the kind most success stories quietly leave out because it isn't as exciting to tell. If you've ever wondered whether it's worth trying again after something didn't work the first time — this book was written for exactly that question.

## INTRODUCTION

We tend to tell success stories backward.

We meet someone at the moment they've already made it — the stable marriage, the thriving business, the finished dream — and we assume, because that's the only chapter we're seeing, that the story must have gone in a straight line to get there. It rarely does. Most of the meaningful things in a person's life were not first-try successes. They were second, third, sometimes tenth attempts, quietly rebuilt after the first version fell apart.

This book is about that quieter, less flattering part of the story — the part usually left out of the highlight reel. It is built entirely on ordinary people's stories, not celebrity ones, because ordinary persistence is actually the more honest teacher. Celebrity stories tend to flatten into simple narratives — "they never gave up" — that skip past the specific, unglamorous mechanics of what an actual retry looks like: the humility of admitting the first attempt failed, the discomfort of trying again in front of people who watched you fail the first time, the very real risk that this attempt might not work either.

Each story in this book follows the same honest shape: an attempt, a failure, an uncomfortable gap, and a second attempt that finally worked — or, in some cases, is still being made, because not every retry has resolved yet by the time this book was written, and that's true to life too.

If there is one thing this book hopes to leave you with, it's a simple reframe: **a failed attempt is not proof that something can't work. It's proof that the first version of your effort wasn't the final one.** The next try might be. You often don't find out which try is the one that works until you've already made it.

## APPENDIX A — A Note on the Stories in This Book
The stories in this book are drawn from ordinary, composite experiences rather than named public figures, in order to keep the focus on the pattern of persistence itself rather than on any individual's private life or public reputation.

## APPENDIX B — Structure
This book is organized around two areas where retries matter most: relationships (Love Edition material) and personal/career pursuits (Life Edition material). Readers are encouraged to notice which category their own current "stuck" situation falls into before applying the book's principles.

## APPENDIX C — Reflection Questions
1. What have you stopped trying, that you quietly still wish had worked?
2. What would it look like to make one more attempt — not the same attempt again, but a wiser version of it?
