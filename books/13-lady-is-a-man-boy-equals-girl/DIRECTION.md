# Direction — Lady Is a Man, Boy = Girl

The provocative working title is part of the project's attention-grabbing concept, but the manuscript must make the meaning clear through substance: equal humanity does not mean identical bodies, personalities, roles, histories or individual abilities. It means neither sex should be treated as inherently more valuable, more innocent, or less accountable.

The book should also address relationships. A boy should be allowed to reject a girl who does not match his values, standards or boundaries. Attraction or sexual opportunity is not automatically a good opportunity. Consent, compatibility, respect, responsibility and self-control should be treated as human issues rather than one-sided rules.

Do not turn this into an attack on women. Do not turn it into an attack on men. The target is the double standard itself.
