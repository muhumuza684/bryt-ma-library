# Lady Is a Man, Boy = Girl

**Subtitle:** Equal Worth, Equal Responsibility, Equal Humanity

**Status:** Concept Locked — manuscript not started

## Core Concept
A book about human equality between boys and girls, men and women: boys are equally deserving of love, care, protection, education, dignity and emotional support; girls are not automatically morally good simply because they are girls; boys can also fail, be vulnerable, need help, and need boundaries. The book should challenge double standards while preserving respect for both sexes.
