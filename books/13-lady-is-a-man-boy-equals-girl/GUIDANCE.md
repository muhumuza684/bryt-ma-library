# Guidance — Lady Is a Man, Boy = Girl

Important framing:
- Boys need affection and care too.
- Girls can succeed and fail; boys can succeed and fail.
- Individual character matters more than stereotypes.
- Rejecting a relationship can be healthy when based on compatibility and boundaries.
- Sexual opportunity is not automatically a positive outcome.
- Standards should apply in both directions.
- Equality does not require pretending men and women are physically or socially identical.
- Avoid contemptuous language about either sex.
- Use real research for claims about gender, education, relationships, violence, health, or social outcomes.
