# Generation Guide — Lady Is a Man, Boy = Girl

Future chapters can be organized around contrasts:
- the boy who needs affection;
- the girl who is not automatically the right partner;
- rejection in both directions;
- standards in dating;
- sexual pressure and self-control;
- failure and second chances;
- education and ambition;
- fathers and sons;
- mothers and sons;
- mothers and daughters;
- fathers and daughters;
- responsibility without humiliation.

The book should leave the reader with reciprocal dignity, not a new hierarchy.
