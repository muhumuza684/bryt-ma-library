# Development Status

## Current state

This project is **Concept Locked**.

The next development stage is chapter architecture. The manuscript should
focus on equal human worth and equal responsibility without turning the
subject into gender-war rhetoric.

## Required next-stage work

1. Define the central argument and narrative structure.
2. Build the chapter sequence.
3. Research factual social, legal, historical, and statistical claims.
4. Distinguish equality of human worth from claims of identical roles,
   bodies, abilities, or circumstances.
5. Ensure examples apply principles consistently to boys/girls and
   men/women.
6. Only then begin manuscript drafting.
