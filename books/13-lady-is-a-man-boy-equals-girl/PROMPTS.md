# Prompts — Lady Is a Man, Boy = Girl

MASTER WRITING PROMPT

Write this book as a balanced exploration of equal human worth and reciprocal standards. Use stories of boys and girls, men and women, showing moments where social expectations become unequal. Give both sides interiority. Let readers see how a boy can be hurt, rejected, neglected, confused, pressured or emotionally exhausted without making him weak. Let readers see how girls can also make harmful choices or fail without turning that into an attack on women. Treat consent, boundaries, attraction, rejection and responsibility as mutual human principles. Avoid gender-war rhetoric.

SCENE PROMPT

Create a believable scene in which the same behavior is judged differently depending on whether a boy or girl does it. Do not tell the reader what to think. Show the reactions, consequences and contradictions, then allow the reflection to emerge naturally.
