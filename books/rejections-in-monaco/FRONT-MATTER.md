# REJECTIONS IN MONACO
## *Eddie Mutwe: Unknown*

### Author's note

Some stories begin with a suitcase.

This one begins with a boy who does not have much to put in one.

Eddie's world starts in Mbarara, where money can be seen without being counted, where clothes can say things before a person opens his mouth, and where a university campus can make two people from very different lives feel as though they are standing on different sides of the same road.

Then comes a rejection.

Then a scholarship.

Then an airport.

Then a country whose language Eddie does not speak.

The road keeps moving.

China teaches him that being accepted into a room is not the same as knowing how to live inside it. Travel teaches him that the world is larger than the place where he was first told what he could become. Monaco shows him a version of wealth and status that once seemed impossibly distant.

But the final test is not whether Eddie can enter the rooms that once ignored him.

It is what he does when he finally has the key.

### Publication safeguard

This is a fictionalized literary work, not a verified biography of the real Eddie Mutwe / Edward Ssebuufu. The plot events involving Acheng, Rich Elwelu, Janet, the scholarship journey, Monaco, and the future governor office must not be marketed as documented biography.
