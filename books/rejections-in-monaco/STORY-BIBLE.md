# STORY BIBLE

## Eddie
Poor university student → rejected → scholarship recipient → foreign student → learner → trusted worker → traveller → internationally exposed adult → powerful Ugandan public figure in the fictional future → tested by revenge → chooses mercy.

## Acheng
Stylish, socially confident and from a more comfortable background. She rejects Eddie, but she is not a cartoon villain. Her decision should emerge through believable social and personal pressures.

## Rich Elwelu
Year 3 Computer Science finalist. Cars, confidence, status and a clearer-looking future make him Eddie's social contrast. He is not required to sabotage Eddie.

## Janet
Acheng and Rich's daughter in the fictional future. She is innocent of the adults' history.

## Monaco
A real place, not a fictional institution. The rejection is social/network/status exclusion, not a law against poor people.

## Motifs
Three shirts; sweater; small bag; passport; departure board; train window; hotel door; sea and hills; file; stamp.

## Voice
Plain sentences. Cinematic physical detail. Objects carry emotion. Real geography where verified. No invented quotes from real people. Continuous narrative. Lessons emerge from events.
