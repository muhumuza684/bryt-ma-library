# Chapter 3: The Scholarship

The scholarship notice did not look like a miracle.

It looked like a notice.

There was an application process, documents, deadlines, academic requirements, and a destination that sounded impossibly far away.

China.

The Chinese Government Scholarship had a history in Uganda going back decades. For qualifying students, scholarship packages could cover major study costs and international airfare. That mattered to Eddie because the hardest part of dreaming was not always the dream itself. Sometimes it was the cost of reaching the first airport.

He found the forms.

He read them more than once.

He collected documents.

He asked questions.

He waited.

Waiting became another kind of test. Every email felt important. Every silence felt like rejection returning in a different uniform.

Then the answer came.

He had qualified.

For several minutes he did not tell anyone.

He sat with the message open on his phone.

China was no longer a country in a documentary. It was a destination.

He began imagining what he would need.

Clothes.

Shoes.

Luggage.

Money.

The list was longer than his resources.

But for the first time in a long time, the future had given him something more useful than sympathy.

It had given him a direction.

> **Pull quote:** A direction can be worth more than sympathy.
