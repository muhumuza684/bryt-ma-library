# Chapter 11: The Man Who Was Still Unknown

There came a point when Eddie stopped checking whether Acheng had noticed his progress.

He had not planned for that moment.

It simply happened.

His attention moved from proving something to building something.

He learned how introductions worked in unfamiliar rooms.

He learned to explain his background without apologising for it.

He learned that the world contained people who had never heard of Mbarara and did not need to.

That was not an insult.

It was freedom.

Eddie had spent years measuring himself through the eyes of people who already knew his history.

Now he was meeting people who knew nothing.

They judged the person standing in front of them.

Sometimes they liked him.

Sometimes they did not.

Sometimes he succeeded.

Sometimes he failed.

For the first time, rejection was no longer connected to one girl's answer.

It had become part of the wider world.

And the wider world was big enough that one closed door could not define the whole journey.

> **Pull quote:** Freedom begins when one person's opinion stops being the measurement of your future.
