# Chapter 5: The Other Side of the World

China met Eddie with distance before it met him with opportunity.

The airport announcements seemed to arrive in a language that had no relationship with the sounds in his head. Signs carried characters he could not read. People moved quickly.

Then came the weather.

The cold entered his body through places he had never thought to protect.

His sweater suddenly became important.

Campus life was another adjustment. The dormitory was not Uganda. Food was not Uganda. Even the silence between strangers felt different.

Eddie learned small survival routines.

How to find the right building.

How to use transport.

How to buy food without having the right words.

How to watch what other students did before trying it himself.

At night he called home.

The call was short because he did not want anyone to hear the loneliness in his voice.

He said he was fine.

Then he ended the call and stared at the ceiling.

The scholarship had carried him across the world.

It had not carried his mind for him.

That part of the journey had to be done alone.

> **Pull quote:** A scholarship can carry a person across the world without carrying away loneliness.
