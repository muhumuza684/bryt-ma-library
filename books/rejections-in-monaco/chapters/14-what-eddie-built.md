# Chapter 14: What Eddie Built

After Monaco, Eddie stopped chasing appearances.

He still cared about presentation.

But he cared more about substance.

He learned to build relationships slowly.

He learned that money could open some doors but could not manufacture trust.

He learned that competence could travel.

A project completed in one country could lead to a conversation in another.

A person helped today could remember you years later.

A failed negotiation could become knowledge.

A mistake could become a system.

The boy who once counted shirts now counted something else: responsibilities.

Who depended on him?

What had he promised?

What could he deliver?

What would happen if he failed?

Power, Eddie discovered, was not simply having money or being recognised.

Power was the ability to affect what happened next.

That made it more dangerous.

And more important.

> **Pull quote:** Power begins where your decisions start changing what happens to other people.
