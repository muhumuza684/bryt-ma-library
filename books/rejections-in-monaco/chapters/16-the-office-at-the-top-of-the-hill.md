# Chapter 16: The Office at the Top of the Hill

The fictional future office stood above a city that had taught Eddie how quickly status could change.

By then, people called him important.

He did not always like the word.

The governor's office in this story is an imagined future political structure, not a claim about Uganda's present constitutional arrangement.

The work was less glamorous than the title.

Budgets.

Roads.

Hospitals.

Staff.

Complaints.

Competing interests.

People asking for favours.

People accusing him of ignoring them.

Every decision created another person who thought he had decided wrongly.

Eddie discovered a new kind of rejection.

It came from people who had supported him and now disagreed with him.

It came from people who wanted something he could not give.

It came from people who believed power had made him unreachable.

He remembered the boy who had once stood outside rooms.

Now he was the person holding the schedule.

That frightened him more than he admitted.

> **Pull quote:** The title is easier to carry than the responsibility beneath it.
