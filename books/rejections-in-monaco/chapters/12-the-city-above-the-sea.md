# Chapter 12: The City Above the Sea

Monaco appeared differently from the Uganda Eddie remembered.

The sea was close.

The hills rose behind the built-up streets.

Port Hercule held boats that looked less like transport and more like floating architecture.

Around Monte-Carlo, polished surfaces, expensive cars, hotels, restaurants, and tightly packed luxury created a visual language Eddie had seen before only through screens.

He walked slowly.

Not because he was lost.

Because he was looking.

Monaco was small.

Its wealth was not.

Eddie thought about the three shirts.

He remembered the sweater.

He remembered the airport.

He remembered the rooms where he had once measured his worth by who was sitting inside.

Here, the question was different.

Who knew you?

Who could introduce you?

Who trusted you?

Who had your name in their phone?

The city did not have to reject him.

It only had to continue without noticing him.

That was enough to make him feel the old distance again.

> **Pull quote:** A city can be beautiful and still make you feel invisible.
