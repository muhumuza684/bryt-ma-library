# Chapter 13: The Room They Did Not Prepare for Him

The invitation was real.

So was the room.

The problem was that Eddie did not know what the room expected.

His clothes were good now.

His shoes were good.

His passport had taken him across borders.

But confidence in one room did not automatically transfer to another.

A conversation moved around him.

Names appeared that he did not know.

People referred to places he had never visited and institutions he had only read about.

Nobody insulted him.

Nobody told him to leave.

He simply felt the invisible border.

When the evening ended, Eddie walked outside.

The Mediterranean air was cool.

The lights on the hills looked close enough to touch.

He could have spent the night being angry.

Instead he wrote down the names of the people he had met.

He searched the organisations they represented.

He studied the work.

He realised the room had not been the whole world.

It had only been one room.

He did not need to conquer it.

He needed to build something that could stand without it.

> **Pull quote:** One room is never the whole world.
