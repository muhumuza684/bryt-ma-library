# Chapter 9: The First Time Eddie Was Trusted

The first real responsibility Eddie received was not glamorous.

It was a task.

Someone needed a person who would do it correctly and return when it was finished.

Eddie accepted.

He checked the details twice.

He finished.

Then he reported back.

Nobody clapped.

Nobody gave him a trophy.

But the next time there was work, someone called him.

That second call mattered more.

Eddie began to understand how reputation is built.

Not in speeches.

In small repetitions.

He became known as the person who could be trusted with a task.

That reputation travelled farther than he expected.

A classmate introduced him to another person.

That person knew another.

The world was opening through ordinary human connections.

Eddie thought about Acheng sometimes.

But less often.

The strange thing about growth was that the old rejection remained in memory while losing its control over the future.

> **Pull quote:** Reputation grows quietly, then suddenly opens a door.
