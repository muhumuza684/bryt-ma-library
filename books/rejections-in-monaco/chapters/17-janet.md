# Chapter 17: Janet

Janet arrived in Eddie's life through a file.

Her name was typed near the top.

The request was formal.

The problem was urgent.

Then Eddie saw the family details.

Acheng.

Rich.

For a moment the office disappeared.

He was back in Mbarara.

Three shirts.

A quiet conversation.

The answer.

The car.

The feeling of being the poorer man in a room.

Now the people from that room were asking for help.

Janet had done nothing.

That was the first thing Eddie understood.

Whatever had happened between adults years ago did not belong to her.

He read the medical request again.

He asked what was needed.

He asked what could legally be done.

He asked which institution had authority.

The staff waited.

Power had brought the past back to his desk.

Now he had to decide what to do with it.

> **Pull quote:** The innocent should not pay for the history of adults.
