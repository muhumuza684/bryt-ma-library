# CHAPTER OUTLINE

## 01. The Boy With Three Shirts

Mbarara, poverty, three shirts, Social Sciences, Acheng, and Rich establish the social gap.
## 02. The Answer Acheng Gave Him

Eddie declares his feelings. Acheng rejects him through a believable mixture of social class, appearance, status and attraction.
## 03. The Scholarship

The scholarship becomes a practical escape route. Eddie applies, waits, and qualifies.
## 04. The Sweater

He packs little, protects the sweater, reaches Entebbe and flies to China.
## 05. The Other Side of the World

China is experienced physically: language, cold, campus, food, transport, dormitory and homesickness.
## 06. The Name They Could Not Pronounce

Academic language creates embarrassment. Eddie learns to treat correction as information.
## 07. The Price of Being Poor

Scholarship support does not erase small expenses or social inequality. Eddie learns discipline.
## 08. A Door With No Handle

He searches for opportunity and discovers that reliability can create doors when status cannot.
## 09. The First Time Eddie Was Trusted

A small responsibility becomes the first real foundation of Eddie's reputation.
## 10. The World Beyond the Campus

Travel across China expands Eddie's map and reduces the power of his original rejection.
## 11. The Man Who Was Still Unknown

Eddie stops trying to prove Acheng wrong and begins building an identity of his own.
## 12. The City Above the Sea

Monaco is shown through real geography, luxury, sea, hills and social contrast.
## 13. The Room They Did Not Prepare for Him

An elite room does not insult Eddie, but he discovers the invisible border of networks and status.
## 14. What Eddie Built

He builds outside elite approval through competence, relationships and responsibility.
## 15. Entebbe Again

Entebbe becomes a mirror: Uganda is familiar, but Eddie is no longer the same.
## 16. The Office at the Top of the Hill

The fictional governor office introduces responsibility, criticism, requests and the danger of power.
## 17. Janet

Janet's medical problem brings Acheng and Rich back into Eddie's world.
## 18. The Stamp

Eddie uses lawful process to help Janet. The story ends with mercy rather than revenge.
