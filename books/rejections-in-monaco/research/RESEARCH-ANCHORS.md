# RESEARCH ANCHORS

## Chinese Government Scholarship
The Chinese Embassy in Uganda states that China has provided scholarships for Ugandan students since the 1980s. Official notices for 2018/19, 2020/21, 2021/22 and 2025/26 describe scholarship arrangements for eligible Ugandan students; the 2018/19 and 2020/21 notices specifically describe tuition, accommodation, living allowance, medical coverage and international airfare.

Sources:
- https://ug.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/sgsw/201801/t20180129_6942358.htm
- https://ug.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/sgsw/202001/t20200108_6942382.htm
- https://ug.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/sgsw/202012/t20201211_6942450.htm
- https://ug.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/xwdt/202412/t20241220_11510192.htm

These sources establish the scholarship framework, not Eddie's fictional award.

## Real Eddie Mutwe
Public sources identify Edward Ssebuufu as a real Ugandan public figure commonly known as Eddie Mutwe. Uganda Judiciary records also use “Edward Ssebuufu alias Eddie Mutwe.”
