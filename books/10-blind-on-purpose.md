# Blind on Purpose

## Concept
A wisdom/discipline book about deliberately choosing not to react to, respond to, or hold onto everything you see, hear, or experience — "pretending" to be blind, deaf, mute, forgetful, or slow — as a strategy for living longer, keeping peace, and ultimately seeing/experiencing more of life. Rooted in proverbial wisdom traditions ("a wise man overlooks an insult"), reframed as *strategic, chosen* limitation rather than actual limitation — a deliberate discipline, not a deficiency.

## Locked Title & Cover
- **Main title:** Blind on Purpose
- **Subtitle:** The Wisdom of What You Choose Not to See
- **Cover tagline:** "The hours no one claps for are the ones that make you."

Other title candidates considered and set aside: *Selective Blindness: See Less, Live Longer* (repurposed as a strong tagline/pull-quote rather than the title).

## Locked Five-Pillar Structure
The first four pillars are about RESTRAINT (what you choose not to react to); the fifth pillar is about INITIATIVE (what you choose to do while others rest) — together forming a complete picture of discipline. This restraint/initiative contrast is a deliberate structural device for the book's rhythm.

### Pillar 1 — Pretend to Be Deaf
Not reacting to every word said about you — gossip, insults, criticism.

### Pillar 2 — Pretend to Be Mute
Not responding to every argument, not needing the last word.

### Pillar 3 — Pretend to Be Forgetful
Letting go of offenses instead of keeping score.

### Pillar 4 — Pretend to Be Slow
Resisting the urge to rush and prove yourself first.

### Pillar 5 — Wake While They Sleep / The Hours No One Claps For
Working, praying, and reading in the hours others give up to sleep, so that effort — and prayer — stand out precisely because they were unseen. Ties thematically to the *Die to Live* book's theme of dying to comfort/ease in order to live more fully. This is the chapter title locked in: **"The Hours No One Claps For."**
