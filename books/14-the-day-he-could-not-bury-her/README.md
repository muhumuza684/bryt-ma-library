# The Day He Could Not Bury Her

**Subtitle:** A Waiswa Mufumbiro Story — Working Title

**Status:** Concept Locked — story research required before manuscript

## Core Concept
A story concept centered on Waiswa Mufumbiro and his departed wife, with the emotional circumstance that he was in prison and was unable to attend or participate in burying her. The book should explore separation, grief, helplessness, love, imprisonment, time, memory, and the strange cruelty of being alive while unable to perform a final duty for someone you love.
