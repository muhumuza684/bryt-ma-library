## Current factual foundation

**Working title:** The Day He Could Not Bury Her  
**Subject:** Alex Waiswa Mufumbiro  
**Wife:** Edith Katende Mufumbiro

### Verified chronology from published reporting

- Edith Katende Mufumbiro died on April 8, 2026 after a prolonged battle
  with cancer.
- At the time of her death, Alex Waiswa Mufumbiro was on remand at
  Luzira Upper Prison.
- Mufumbiro sought temporary release/bail so that he could attend his
  wife's burial.
- His request was not granted before the burial.
- Edith was buried in Luuka District on April 11, 2026.

## Writing safeguards

This is a real-person, real-event project.

Do not invent:
- private conversations
- internal thoughts
- undocumented memories
- exact dialogue
- unverified motives
- private marital details
- prison scenes
- medical details not supported by sources

When a source makes a claim, attribute it to the source rather than adopting
a contested claim as established fact.

The eventual manuscript should state clearly whether it is:
1. factual nonfiction,
2. fact-anchored literary nonfiction, or
3. explicitly fictionalized/literary reconstruction.

Until that decision is made, the project remains a research/documentation
project rather than a completed manuscript.
