# Guidance — The Day He Could Not Bury Her

Research checklist before manuscript:
- Confirm full identity and spelling.
- Confirm the wife’s identity and relationship.
- Confirm dates and places.
- Establish the imprisonment period and relevant legal context.
- Verify whether he was legally or practically prevented from attending.
- Seek interviews, statements, family accounts, court records or reputable reporting where available.
- Mark every uncertain detail.

Writing rule: never invent private thoughts and attribute them to Waiswa as fact. They can be rendered as literary reconstruction only if the book is clearly labeled accordingly.
