# Direction — The Day He Could Not Bury Her

This concept must be handled as a fact-anchored project if it is intended to portray a real Waiswa Mufumbiro. Before drafting as nonfiction, verify the identity, imprisonment, relationship, death, dates, location, burial circumstances and all other biographical claims from reliable sources and, where possible, first-person or family testimony.

Do not fill missing details with imagination and present them as facts. If the evidence is incomplete, the project can later become an explicitly fictionalized or literary reconstruction, but that boundary must be declared.

The emotional center is not spectacle. It is the question: what does a person do with love and grief when time, law, distance or imprisonment prevents the final goodbye?
