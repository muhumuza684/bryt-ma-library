# Prompts — The Day He Could Not Bury Her

RESEARCH PROMPT

Research Waiswa Mufumbiro and the death and burial of his wife. Build a source table with: claim, date, source, exact evidence, confidence, and unresolved questions. Prioritize primary records, direct interviews, family statements, court records and reputable Ugandan reporting.

LITERARY PROMPT

Once the factual record is established, write scenes with restraint. Use objects, places, silence, phone calls, prison routines, funeral preparations and distance to carry emotion. Do not manufacture quotations. Where the record is silent, remain silent or clearly mark reconstruction.
