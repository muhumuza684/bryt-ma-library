# When Time Comes

**Subtitle:** The Moment the Spirit Rises

**Status:** Concept Locked — manuscript not started

## Core Concept
A spiritual and experiential book about a moment when a person feels spiritually awakened or strengthened: the sense that the body is responding to an inner spiritual force, producing unusual endurance, focus, courage, discipline or capacity to keep working. The book explores the experience without claiming that every spiritual interpretation is scientifically proven.
