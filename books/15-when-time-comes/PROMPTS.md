# Prompts — When Time Comes

MASTER WRITING PROMPT

Write 'When Time Comes' as a reflective spiritual literary work. Begin with a person at an ordinary low point. Show the small event, realization, prayer, conversation or inner decision that changes the person's relationship with effort. Let the transformation unfold through behavior rather than grand declarations. Describe the body, surroundings, silence, breath, work and time. Keep spiritual interpretation honest: what the character experiences can be deeply real to them without being presented as laboratory proof.

RESEARCH PROMPT

Where the manuscript discusses psychology, physiology, sleep, stress, exercise, spirituality or altered states, distinguish peer-reviewed evidence, historical religious teachings, personal testimony and author interpretation.
