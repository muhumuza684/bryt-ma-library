# Guidance — When Time Comes

Explore:
- spiritual awakening;
- prayer and conviction;
- purpose;
- discipline;
- endurance;
- moments of extraordinary effort;
- the relationship between mind, body and belief;
- fatigue and recovery;
- the danger of glorifying exhaustion;
- spiritual experiences across different traditions without declaring one tradition universally correct.

If scientific claims are later added, research them separately. Never use spirituality to encourage unsafe overwork or to dismiss illness, sleep needs, nutrition, or medical care.
