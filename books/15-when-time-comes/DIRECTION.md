# Direction — When Time Comes

The central idea is timing: there are moments when preparation, conviction, faith, purpose, experience and spiritual belief seem to meet, and a person enters a different state of effort.

The manuscript should distinguish personal spiritual testimony from medical or scientific claims. It can describe how people experience spiritual strength, prayer, conviction, purpose, flow, endurance or renewed energy, while avoiding unsupported claims that supernatural power has been scientifically demonstrated.

The title should feel like a threshold: 'when time comes' is the moment a person recognizes that something inside them has changed.
