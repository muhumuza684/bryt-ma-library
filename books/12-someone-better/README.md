# Someone Better

**Subtitle:** There Is Always Someone Better Than You

**Status:** Concept Locked — manuscript not started

## Core Concept
A book about accepting a difficult but useful truth: in almost every field of life, there will be someone ahead of you in some ability, opportunity, wealth, influence, beauty, knowledge, discipline, connections, or achievement. The point is not humiliation or surrender. The book should explore how people respond when they discover they are not the strongest person in the room, and how acknowledgement of other people's strengths can produce humility, learning, cooperation, discipline, and better leadership.
