# Guidance — Someone Better

Core themes:
- Relative strength: people differ by domain.
- Humility without self-erasure.
- Learning from people ahead of you.
- The danger of believing position equals superiority.
- Leadership as stewardship rather than personal supremacy.
- How institutions improve when people can acknowledge expertise.
- The difference between being the best available and being the best imaginable.
- How technology, education, business and sport expose comparative ability.

Do not use the book to make unsupported claims about specific political leaders. If real leaders or institutions are discussed later, research and attribute every factual claim.
