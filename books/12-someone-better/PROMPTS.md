# Prompts — Someone Better

MASTER WRITING PROMPT

Write a chapter for 'Someone Better' as a serious, readable literary nonfiction book. Start with a concrete human situation rather than a slogan. Let the reader experience the moment when someone discovers another person is better at a particular thing. Build the idea through action, dialogue, contrast, and reflection. Never imply that a person's total human worth can be ranked. Make 'better' specific to the skill, situation, opportunity, or decision being discussed. Avoid generic motivational language and avoid preaching. End with a realization that naturally grows from the story.

RESEARCH PROMPT

For any real-world example, identify the people/institutions, date, location, primary or authoritative source, and what exactly is documented. Separate documented fact from interpretation. Do not manufacture examples.
