# SOURCES — The Chokepoint

Research notes and grounding facts for the three anonymized decision-making scenarios. **These notes are for the author's reference only and should never appear in the published book** — the book's entire value depends on keeping the real-world names and events out of the text.

---

## Scenario 1 — "The Chokepoint" (grounded in Iran / Israel / USA tension)

**Key facts gathered:**
- A 12-Day War occurred in June 2025, ending in a ceasefire.
- A fuller campaign began February 28, 2026, involving Israel and the US against Iran — included the killing of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei and strikes on nuclear/military infrastructure.
- A Pakistan-mediated ceasefire and a June memorandum halted large-scale fighting.
- Both sides continued trading strikes over alleged violations after the ceasefire.
- Core unresolved disputes: Iran's nuclear program, the Strait of Hormuz, sanctions relief.

**Key lesson extracted for the book:** the miscalculation was not about timeline — it was underestimating how unresolved the underlying conflict would remain even after a decisive strike and a leadership kill. Power vacuums and grievances don't end just because the initial military objective was hit.

**Research status:** Verified via web search during initial project development (Sept 2026). Re-verify before final publication, as this is an active/evolving situation.

## Scenario 2 — "The Border" (grounded in Russia / Ukraine)

**Key facts gathered:**
- Now in its fifth year; the largest military conflict in Europe since WWII.
- UK Ministry of Defense assessment (May 2026): Russia sustained 1,000,000+ casualties for approximately 1% of Ukrainian territory gained.
- Analyst assessment: the primary ongoing risk is unintended escalation through miscalculation, not intentional escalation.

**Key lesson extracted for the book:** initial capability assessments almost always underestimate the will of the side fighting for its own existence versus the side fighting for territory or ideology.

**Research status:** Verified via web search (Sept 2026). This conflict remains active — re-verify casualty and territory figures before publication, as these change over time.

## Scenario 3 — "The Island" (grounded in Taiwan / China)

**Key facts gathered:**
- A 2026 US intelligence report found the regional rising power is not committed to a specific-year invasion.
- Factors weighed: PLA/military readiness, Taiwan's own actions, and whether the US would intervene.
- Taiwan's defense leadership has stated the deterrence goal plainly: make any invasion attempt carry high enough risk that the calculation keeps pushing the timeline back.

**Key lesson extracted for the book:** this is the cleanest example of "a decision that hasn't been made yet" — deterrence working in real time, unlike the other two scenarios.

**Research status:** Verified via web search (Sept 2026). This is the most fluid of the three situations — re-verify closest to publication date, since intelligence assessments and public statements can shift quickly.
