# SOURCES — God of Balimwezo (All Three Volumes)

Research notes and grounding facts for the real, named individuals featured across this series. All claims below were verified via web search during initial project development (Sept 2026) — re-verify anything time-sensitive before final publication, since political roles and situations can change.

---

## Ronald Balimwezo (central figure, all three volumes)
- Won Kampala's Lord Mayor race in January 2026 on the NUP ticket, ending Erias Lukwago's 15-year tenure.
- Sworn in as Lord Mayor in May 2026.
- Publicly thanked Bobi Wine for "believing in me and giving me a chance."
- Background: civil engineer and lecturer at Kyambogo University; in local government since 2006.
- Career path: Nakawa Division mayor (2011–2021) → MP (2021–2026) → Lord Mayor of Kampala (2026).

## Joel Ssenyonyi (Volume 2 — The Door Bobi Wine Opened)
- Leader of Opposition (LOP), 12th Parliament, in role since January 2024 (succeeded Mathias Mpuuga).
- Came in as spokesperson for People Power (Bobi Wine's movement) before it became NUP.
- Won the Nakawa West parliamentary seat in 2021.
- Background: journalist before entering politics.

## Ali Bukeni / "Nubian Li" (Volume 3 — Kingmaker)
- Singer and long-time creative partner of Bobi Wine.
- Declared Nakawa Division Mayor in January 2026, defeating incumbent Paul Mugambe.
- At his 2021 wedding, Bobi Wine publicly stated that without Nubian Li's encouragement, he might never have contested the 2017 Kyadondo East by-election that started his own political career.
- Was jailed for nearly a year following the 2021 campaigns.
- His wife, Mutoni Gloria, won a local council seat during that time and was later appointed Deputy Mayor of Nakawa Division.

## Hussein Muyonjo / "Swengere" (Volume 3 — Kingmaker)
- Comedian and radio presenter (Baba FM), well known across eastern Uganda.
- No prior political career before this race.
- Won the NUP ticket for Jinja North Division ahead of 2026 — the sitting MP withdrew and endorsed him.
- Won the general seat, defeating 13 other candidates, with 19,427 votes.
