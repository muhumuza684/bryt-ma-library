# SOURCES — She Da Moon

Research notes and grounding facts for the four featured couples. All claims below were verified via web search during initial project development (Sept 2026) — re-verify anything time-sensitive before final publication.

---

## Barbie Itungo Kyagulanyi & Bobi Wine (Uganda)
- Met in her teens; together roughly 10 years before marrying in 2011.
- She has remained his only wife through his rise from musician to opposition leader.
- Built her own independent identity: author, founder of Caring Hearts Uganda.

## Janet Kataaha Museveni & Yoweri Museveni (Uganda)
- Married in 1973.
- Walked through exile with him (Tanzania, Kenya, Sweden) before he held power.
- Has remained his only wife through decades in government.
- Built her own long public record: Minister of Education, founder of UWESO.

## Peng Liyuan & Xi Jinping (China)
- Married since 1987; one marriage, no reported scandal.
- Peng Liyuan is a famous folk singer in her own right, later a UN goodwill ambassador.

## Nita & Mukesh Ambani (India)
- Married in 1985; introduction arranged by Mukesh's father.
- Widely described as one of the most stable, mutually respectful marriages among global business figures.
- Nita runs Reliance Foundation and is a major cultural figure in her own right; Mukesh runs Reliance Industries.
- Anecdote (verify sourcing before using directly in manuscript): Mukesh reportedly proposed at a stoplight and refused to drive on until she said yes.

## Rejected Example — Note for the Record
An earlier candidate example (a story suggesting a young Putin was rejected by a woman who chose a teacher over him, but that he continued to respect her) was researched and **could not be verified** — no credible source supports this specific narrative. What the record actually shows is closer to the opposite: Putin married Lyudmila Shkrebneva in 1983 after roughly four years together; the marriage was reportedly troubled and ended in divorce in 2013, with multiple independent reports since linking him to other relationships during and after the marriage. This example was dropped and replaced with the Ambanis. **Do not reintroduce this example without new, credible sourcing.**

## Research Standards for This Book (per Master Guide)
- Do not include any claim about a real person's relationship, marriage, or personal life that isn't publicly documented.
- If a story sounds dramatic, convenient, or too good to be true, verify it especially hard — see the Putin note above as the cautionary example.
- Two of the four couples (Barbie/Bobi Wine, Janet/Museveni) sit on opposing sides of Uganda's political divide — keep the framing focused on the marriage/devotion principle, not political commentary.
