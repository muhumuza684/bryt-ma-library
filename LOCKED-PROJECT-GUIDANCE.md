# LOCKED PROJECT GUIDANCE

These concepts are recorded before manuscript production.

General rule:
- Preserve the original idea.
- Improve structure without silently changing the thesis.
- Separate documented fact from interpretation.
- Do not manufacture quotations, statistics, dates, private thoughts or biographical events.
- Build scenes and examples before explaining the lesson.
- Avoid generic motivational filler.
- Keep titles provocative only when the manuscript can responsibly explain their intended meaning.
- For real people, research first.
- For spiritual claims, distinguish testimony from scientific evidence.
