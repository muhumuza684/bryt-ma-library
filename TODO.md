Status: Concept Locked; research foundation established.

- Wife: Edith Katende Mufumbiro.
- Edith died on April 8, 2026 after a prolonged battle with cancer.
- Mufumbiro was on remand at Luzira Upper Prison.
- He sought temporary release/bail so he could attend and bury his wife.
- His request was not granted before the burial.
- Edith was buried in Luuka District on April 11, 2026.
- Published reports identify the event and provide a starting source trail.

Important research rule:
- The manuscript must distinguish verified facts, attributed statements,
  reported testimony, and literary reconstruction.

  presented as verified fact unless supported by a source.
- Legal claims should be checked against primary court documents or reliable
  reporting before final manuscript publication.
- The project's final form must be explicitly identified as factual,
  fact-anchored literary nonfiction, or fictionalized reconstruction.

Primary research sources currently identified:

- Uganda Radio Network reporting on the request and background.
